Privacy Policy — Veil: Live Wallpaper

Developer: mojux  ·  Package: com.mojux.livewallpaper.veil

Last updated: October 16, 2026

English日本語

1. Overview

Veil: Live Wallpaper (“the App”) is a live wallpaper for Android developed by mojux. The App itself does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, store or transmit any personal information to the developer.

The App does, however, include third-party software (SDKs) for advertising and analytics that collect certain data on their own. This policy explains what those are and what you can do about them.

2. Data stored on your device

Your wallpaper settings — background color, style, layout, number of veils, background glow, ripple, speed, detail, edge glow, colors and color change interval, sparkles, smoothness and the choices made through Today’s Veil — are saved in the App’s private storage on your device only. They are not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

3. Data collected by third parties

The App uses the following services. Each collects data under its own privacy policy.

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device information, and interactions with ads To show and measure ads
Firebase Analytics App usage events collected automatically (such as app opens and screen views), an app instance identifier, the advertising ID where available, device model, OS version and approximate location derived from the IP address To understand how the App is used and which features matter
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports (including crashes in the App’s native drawing code), device model, OS version and an installation identifier To find and fix crashes

These services are provided by Google. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase’s privacy information for details on how they handle data.

Where ads appear

  • A banner ad at the bottom of the App’s settings screen.
  • A full-screen ad that may appear when you open the App from the launcher. It is shown at most once in a while, and never while you are choosing a wallpaper.

No ads are shown on the wallpaper itself.

4. Personalized ads and your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows a consent form the first time it runs, provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform. Your choice controls whether ads are personalized.

You can review or change that choice at any time: open the App’s settings screen and tap Privacy options under Information.

Independently of the App, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Doing so limits ad personalization across all apps on the device.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children and is not designed for use by children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. International transfers

The third-party services above are operated by Google and may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States.

7. Your rights and how to reach us

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of data held about you. Because the developer does not hold your data directly, such requests are generally directed to Google for the services listed above. If you have a question or a request concerning the App, contact:

mojux.support+veil@gmail.com

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect in the App.

Veil - 光のベール ライブ壁紙（以下「本アプリ」）は、mojux が開発する Android 向けのライブ壁紙です。 本アプリ自体はアカウント登録を求めず、開発者が個人情報を収集・保存・送信することは ありません。

ただし本アプリには、広告とアナリティクス（利用状況の分析）のために、 独自にデータを収集する第三者製のソフトウェア（SDK）が組み込まれています。 本ポリシーでは、その内容と、利用者が取れる対応を説明します。

壁紙の設定（背景色、表現、構図、ベールの数、背景の光、うねりの大きさ、速さ、細かさ、縁の光り方、 色の選び方と変わる間隔、キラキラ、なめらかさ、「今日のベール」で選んだ内容）は、端末内の本アプリ専用領域にのみ保存されます。 外部に送信されることはなく、アプリを削除すると一緒に消えます。

本アプリは以下のサービスを利用しています。収集はそれぞれのプライバシーポリシーに基づきます。

サービス収集される情報目的
Google AdMob 広告 ID、IP アドレス（これに基づくおおよその位置）、端末情報、広告への操作 広告の配信と効果測定

選択内容はいつでも変更できます。本アプリの設定画面を開き、「情報」欄の プライバシー設定 をタップしてください。
